Privacy Policy & Data Safety Notice

Last Updated: October 06, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Mhare Gaam Ki Rasoi ("we", "our", or "us"). We operate authentic pure vegetarian dining and food delivery services based out of Sai Plaza, Meerut, Uttar Pradesh, India.

This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application and web portals collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your personal information when you use our services. We are dedicated to respecting your privacy in strict compliance with the Information Technology Act, 2000 (India) and Google Play Developer Policies.

2. Information We Collect

To provide high-quality food delivery and table booking services, we collect only the essential information necessary:

  • Contact Information: Your full name, mobile phone number, and delivery address.
  • Order & Dine-In Details: Menu items selected, cooking instructions/spice preferences, table reservation details, and order history.
  • Payment & Transaction Reference: When paying online via UPI, we collect your bank Transaction ID / UTR (Unique Transaction Reference) number provided by you to reconcile your payment with our restaurant records. We do not store or process debit/credit card PINs, CVV codes, or net banking passwords.
  • Device & Network Information: Basic device identifiers, network connection state, and app performance logs to ensure smooth order tracking and push notifications.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information exclusively for legitimate restaurant business operations:

  • To prepare, cook, and pack your traditional chulha food orders.
  • To deliver meals to your designated doorstep or reserve your baithak table.
  • To send you SMS/WhatsApp/Push status notifications regarding your meal preparation and delivery rider progress.
  • To verify UPI payments and maintain accurate billing and tax invoices.
  • To provide customer support and address any feedback or order queries.

4. Data Sharing & Third-Party Disclosure

We do NOT sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties or marketing brokers.

Information is disclosed solely in the following limited circumstances:

  • Delivery Personnel: Your delivery address and phone number are shared with our designated delivery riders solely for order fulfillment.
  • Legal Compliance: When required by applicable Indian laws, judicial orders, or government regulations.

5. Data Security & Storage

We implement administrative, technical, and physical security measures to protect your personal details against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. All communications between the mobile application and our servers use secure protocols.

6. Data Retention and Account Deletion

We retain order history and billing records as required under Indian commercial and tax record retention laws. You have the right to request deletion of your personal data or phone number from our active contact lists at any time by contacting us.

7. Children's Privacy

Our application is not targeted at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors without parental consent.
